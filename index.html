About Me

I’m an Information Technology student at Sudan University of Science and Technology, and most of what I do outside class revolves around cybersecurity and building things that work. I got into security through TryHackMe rooms and capture-the-flag competitions, and it turned into a habit — I still spend a good chunk of my free time in labs, reading write-ups, and picking apart how web apps and networks fail.

On the development side, I work mostly in Python and JavaScript, building small backend services with Flask, working with SQL databases, and putting together full projects end to end — from a Telegram bot for university students to internal tools for managing pharmacy and IT-support workflows. Networking is the other half of the picture: I like understanding how traffic actually moves, not just how to configure it.

I’m still early in my career, and I’m not going to pretend otherwise — but I show up consistently, finish what I start, and treat every lab, CTF, and project as practice for the kind of IT and security work I want to be doing professionally.
